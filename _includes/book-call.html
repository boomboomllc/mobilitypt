Demo

Online booking. Each clinic button opens one of the clinic's own Google Calendar booking pages: one for first visits, one for follow-ups. Bookings land in the calendar already titled ("First visit" or "Follow-up"), and names and times go straight into the clinic's Google account, never through this website. Once the site is live, a clinic without a link just gets call buttons.

To set up: paid Google Workspace with Google's HIPAA agreement (BAA) turned on, Google Calendar as the clinic's real schedule, and booking pages that ask for contact details only.
